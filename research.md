# Research

My research focuses on computational and experimental analysis of composite structures.

## Topics

- Progressive damage in laminated composites
- Delamination initiation and propagation
- Cohesive-zone modeling
- Composite failure criteria
- Nondestructive evaluation
- XCT and ultrasonic inspection
- Curved and L-shaped composite structures
- Data-driven structural characterization
