        <p class="section-label">About</p>
        <h2>Engineering practical composite structures with research-level depth.</h2>
        <p>
          I am a mechanical engineer with a Ph.D. focused on finite-element-based failure analysis of
          composite materials and quantitative NDT characterization. I currently work on fiberglass
          composite product development, structural simulation, laminate optimization, manufacturing
          support, and value engineering.
        </p>
        <p>
          Before moving into industry, my graduate research at Baylor University covered composite
          failure, delamination, cohesive-zone modeling, microstructural characterization, ultrasound,
          X-ray CT, and fiber-orientation quantification. I enjoy turning complex mechanics into designs
          that are safer, lighter, easier to manufacture, and more cost effective.
        </p>
      </div>
