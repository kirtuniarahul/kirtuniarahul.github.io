## About Me

My background combines **composite mechanics, structural simulation, manufacturing, and research**. I work at the intersection of engineering analysis and practical product development, using simulation and testing to improve structural performance, reduce material usage, and support manufacturing decisions.

I earned my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Baylor University, where my research focused on composite structures, finite element modeling, damage and failure analysis, and nondestructive evaluation.

---

## Experience

### Senior Composite Structural Engineer — Latham, The Pool Company

- Structural design and finite element analysis of fiberglass composite products.
- Laminate optimization and value engineering.
- Reinforcement design for critical structural regions.
- Manufacturing process development and automation support.
- New material and process evaluation for future composite systems.

### Research Engineer II — CRES

- Finite element modeling for structural and pipeline applications.
- Nonlinear analysis, loading evaluation, and failure assessment.
- Engineering simulation and interpretation of structural response.

### Graduate Research Assistant — Baylor University

- Composite mechanics, damage modeling, NDT, microscopy, and computational mechanics.
- Finite element analysis using ABAQUS and related engineering software.
- Research on composite failure, delamination, and structural behavior.

## Selected Projects

### Composite Structural Optimization

<img class="project-image" src="{{ '/assets/images/fea-project.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Finite element analysis project">

FEA-driven design optimization of composite structures under realistic loading conditions. The work includes deformation control, reinforcement placement, laminate design, and structural validation before manufacturing.

### Fiberglass Laminate Value Engineering

Optimized laminate architecture to reduce unnecessary material while maintaining required structural performance.

### Polyurethane Process Development

Evaluated glass-reinforced polyurethane systems as a potential pathway for faster curing, process automation, reduced manual rolling, and improved manufacturing throughput.

### Composite Failure Analysis

Applied composite failure criteria, progressive damage modeling, and cohesive-zone methods to investigate failure initiation and delamination in laminated composite structures.
